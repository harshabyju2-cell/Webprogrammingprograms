Services

Three ways we work with coffee.

Whether you're pouring for customers or just for yourself, every relationship starts with a conversation about volume, flavor, and how often you want fresh coffee arriving.

Wholesale roasting

We supply cafés, restaurants, and offices with custom-roasted coffee on a recurring schedule. Every account gets a dedicated contact, consistent roast profiles, and delivery windows built around your service hours.

Typical onboarding: 2–3 weeks, including sample cupping and a trial order.

Included with wholesale accounts

  • Sample cupping before you commit
  • Custom roast profiles for espresso or filter
  • Flexible, recurring delivery schedules
  • Barista training on request

Subscription options

  • Weekly, biweekly, or monthly delivery
  • Single-origin or house blend
  • Whole bean or ground to your brew method
  • Pause or swap anytime

Home subscriptions

Coffee roasted specifically for your delivery date, not pulled from a warehouse shelf. Each bag ships with tasting notes, origin details, and brewing suggestions for the roast you received.

Typical timeline: first bag ships within 3 days of signing up.

Café consulting

For new shops getting started or established cafés refining their program, we help with menu development, equipment selection, and staff training — grounded in what's actually realistic to execute during a rush.

Typical timeline: 4–8 weeks for a full menu and training engagement.

Good fits for consulting

  • Shops opening within the next few months
  • Teams revising an existing coffee menu
  • Owners choosing equipment for the first time
  • Staff needing structured barista training

Not sure which fits your situation?

Send us a note about your business — we'll point you toward the right option.
