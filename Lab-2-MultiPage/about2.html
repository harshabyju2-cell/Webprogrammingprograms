About

Started with a stovetop roaster. Still roasting by hand.

Ember Roasting began in 2014 when founder Daniel Cho started roasting green coffee on a stovetop popcorn popper for friends. The roaster has changed since, but the attention to each batch hasn't.

How we got here

2014

Daniel roasts his first sellable batch on a modified popcorn popper and starts selling bags at a weekend farmers market.

2017

The roastery moves into its current space on Ashok Street and installs its first proper drum roaster.

2021

Wholesale accounts become a full service line after several local cafés ask to switch their house coffee.

2026

A team of six roasts several hundred pounds a week, supplying subscribers, cafés, and offices across the region.

What we believe

Coffee should taste like where it came from. That belief shapes how we buy: we visit farms in person when we can, pay above commodity price, and roast to highlight each origin's character instead of flattening it into a house style.

We'd rather turn down a large order we can't roast fresh in time than ship coffee we're not proud of. That's meant saying no to convenient shortcuts more than once — and it's the reason accounts stick with us for years.
